# Kin Dental Centre — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Kin Dental Centre is a family dental practice in Singapore’s West Coast/Clementi area, providing preventive, restorative, cosmetic and surgical care for patients of different ages. Its website helps patients explore treatments, plan a clinic visit and make contact.

The redesigned homepage prioritises treatment discovery, presents consistent opening hours and provides clearer location guidance.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Earlier Access to Treatments

Six direct treatment pathways now appear immediately after the hero. Previously, older articles appeared ahead of a clear treatment overview. The revised order makes treatment information easier to find near the start of the page.

### 2. A Consistent Opening Schedule

The homepage uses one schedule based on the dedicated location page, with a reminder to confirm before visiting. This addresses conflicting Friday hours across pages and gives patients a clear reference when planning a visit.

### 3. Clearer Location Guidance

The embedded map has been replaced with the clinic’s published Google Maps listing, supported by its address, landmark and parking information. This addresses the previous embed’s location-accuracy warning and provides practical guidance for reaching the clinic.

## Design Improvements

- **Layout and hierarchy:** Treatment pathways follow the hero, giving services earlier prominence.
- **Consistency:** Opening hours follow one identified source.
- **Location presentation:** The map listing, address, landmark and parking details support visit planning.
- **User experience:** Patients can find treatments sooner and access clearer practical information.
