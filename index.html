How do I request an appointment?

Call +65 6979 7978 or WhatsApp +65 8022 1068. Share your reason for visiting and preferred timing. Wait for the clinic to confirm your appointment.

Is this one clinic or two locations?

Kin Dental Centre is at 505 West Coast Drive, #01-214, Singapore 120505. “West Coast / Clementi” describes the area served by this clinic.

Can my child visit Kin?

Kin lists paediatric dentistry among its services. Contact the clinic to discuss your child's needs and arrange a suitable visit. Read about children's dentistry .

Where can I check insurance arrangements?

Visit the clinic's insurance and corporate information page , then confirm your plan and treatment coverage with the clinic before your visit.

What if I do not know which treatment I need?

Tell the team what is bothering you when arranging your appointment. A dental assessment is needed to establish suitable care; you do not need to choose a procedure beforehand.
